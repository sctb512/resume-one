Changes: HTML/CSS/JavaScript replaced by bounded declarative JSON; print tokens,
centered masthead, serif typography and fine rules retained. Item headings use
Resume One's combined semantic labels rather than separate italic organization
lines. Optional portraits and custom .meta settings are not interpreted. CJK
glyphs use the bundled Noto fallback. Word reflows editable content independently.
This is an adaptation, not a pixel-identical rendering of the web theme.
