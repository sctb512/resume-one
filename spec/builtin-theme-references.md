# Layout References for the Built-in Themes

**English** · [简体中文](./builtin-theme-references.zh-CN.md)

The 20 references below were selected from publicly documented layouts in November 2026. GitHub stars measure interest in open-source projects. Resume.io reports template selections, Harvard provides an open career-service template, and European employment services recommend Europass. The two RenderCV references come from official documentation. Resume One redesigns paper, hierarchy, and spacing for each theme and ships all 20 as declarative JSON.

## Historical Popularity Signals

- At the time of research, Awesome-CV had about 28.9k stars, Bill Ryan Resume 11.6k, SB2Nov 7.1k, Deedy Resume 5.1k, Jake’s Resume 2.8k, McDowell CV 2.7k, AltaCV 1.6k, and ModernCV 1.0k. RenderCV had about 17.9k across all its layouts.
- Resume.io reported cumulative selections of about 12 million for Stockholm, 7.6 million Dublin, 5.2 million New York, 3.9 million Toronto, 3.1 million Vienna, 2.7 million Sydney, 2.3 million Amsterdam, and 780,000 Oslo.
